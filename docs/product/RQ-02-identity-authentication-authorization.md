## Goal and scope
Allow a legitimate participant to establish an account, authenticate, retain a controlled authenticated state, obtain permitted information about themselves, and perform only permitted actions. Establish business meaning before choosing data/technology. Registration, identity proof, email/phone control, authentication, session lifecycle, current-user representation, authorization, public profile, legal identity and organization membership are distinct concerns. Authentication success is not permission or proof of legal identity. Public browsing may not require accounts. No confirmed customer/identity policy yet.
