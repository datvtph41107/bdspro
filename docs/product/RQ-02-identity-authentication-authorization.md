## Goal and scope
Allow a legitimate participant to establish an account, authenticate, retain a controlled authenticated state, obtain permitted information about themselves, and perform only permitted actions. Establish business meaning before choosing data/technology. Registration, identity proof, email/phone control, authentication, session lifecycle, current-user representation, authorization, public profile, legal identity and organization membership are distinct concerns. Authentication success is not permission or proof of legal identity. Public browsing may not require accounts. No confirmed customer/identity policy yet.

| ID-01 | Account establishment: who may register, which credentials, required data, uniqueness, verification and activation | valid authorized registration yields usable or pending account per policy; duplicate/racing attempts cannot create conflicting identities; no partial account if atomic creation required |
| ID-02 | Authenticate a registered participant | valid credentials and account state yield authenticated session; invalid credentials fail without disclosing account existence; abuse controls |
| ID-03 | Manage authentication continuity and termination | valid session accepted; expired/revoked session denied; logout effect and multi-device policy explicit; token/cookie handling and transport protection |
| ID-04 | Read one's own account/profile after authentication | identity derived from trusted server authentication; only allowed fields returned; no secret or credential exposure; unauthenticated/invalid session denied |
| ID-05 | Authorization to protected actions/resources | actor/action/resource/scope explicitly evaluated; deny unauthorized and cross-account access, no implicit admin/company privilege |
Account recovery, verification resend and change of credentials need safe policy before public release; MFA, phone, Google OIDC, linking, public profile, organization/team/RBAC and admin capabilities are candidates, not automatic first-slice scope. Email control != legal person verification. No selected JWT/cookie, password algorithm, endpoint or table redesign.
